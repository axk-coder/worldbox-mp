Multiplayer Socket Settings

Select your transport engine to link with local LAN players or online servers:

  • Broadcast LAN: Direct zero-setup multi-tab / local network socket.
  • WebRTC P2P: Peer-to-peer encrypted connection with low latency.
  • WebSocket: Dedicated server relay stream.
  • WebTransport / Polling: Secondary protocol fallback.
